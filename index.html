Origin Story

The Night Reality
Tore Open

Project Threshold was supposed to stabilise a classified wormhole. Instead, its particle accelerator activated meteor material buried beneath the facility and opened a wound into Echo Earth — a warped reflection of our own world. On The Siren Night, prehistoric life, anomaly storms, and broken pieces of reality came through.

Years after humanity fled to space, you awaken in a forgotten cryo facility on an Earth everyone else declared dead.

Guided by the damaged AI Aether, you step into a planet that is hostile, beautiful, and unmistakably alive. Follow the Blackout Archive through abandoned bunkers and military ruins, adapt to the new wilderness, and uncover why Beal and Nathan risked everything to preserve the truth. The goal is not to restore the old Earth. It is to prove humanity can survive the one that remains.

The World

What Awaits Outside

Three forces now shape the new Earth. Learn to survive — and thrive — in all of them.

🌿
Biome

The Living World

Earth changed without humanity. New ecosystems spread through old ruins while surviving wildlife and displaced prehistoric species learn the same dangerous world.

☄️
Anomaly Zone

The Threshold Scars

Near meteor excavations and Project Threshold ruins, anomaly energy still weakens reality. Terrain repeats, gravity slips, and storms carry traces of somewhere that should not exist.

🌀
Dimension

Echo Earth

A damaged reflection of Earth waits beyond stabilised rifts. Its forests, rivers, and ruins look familiar from a distance — and deeply wrong once you step closer.
