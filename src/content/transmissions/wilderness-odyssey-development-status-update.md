# 📢 Wilderness Odyssey — Development Status Update

Hey everyone!

I wanted to give you all a little update on what's been happening with Wilderness Odyssey, why the next update is taking so long, and what my plans are moving forward.

First off, **Wilderness Odyssey is NOT abandoned!** I'm still actively working on the project, and there's been a lot happening behind the scenes.

### 🛠️ Why is the next update taking so long?

One of the biggest reasons is that Wilderness Odyssey has grown into something much bigger than I originally planned.

I'm not just trying to throw together a bunch of mods. My goal is to create an entirely different survival experience inside Minecraft, with exploration, abandoned towns and cities, unique environments, custom mechanics, and an actual storyline.

A lot of this involves developing the **Wilderness Odyssey API**, which is essentially the foundation for many of the custom features I want to add.

The problem is that developing these systems, making sure they're compatible with other mods, and optimizing everything takes quite a bit of time.

I also don't want to release an update that's full of crashes, performance problems, or unfinished systems just for the sake of releasing something.

### 🌎 What's being worked on?

Here's a small look at gameplay so far. In this screenshot you can even spot the bunker in the distance, along with some of the world generation, terrain, and atmosphere I'm working on.

![Wilderness Odyssey gameplay preview showing the landscape, coastline, forested terrain, and the bunker visible in the background.](/images/blog/development-update/world-preview-bunker.png)

There are quite a few things currently being worked on or planned, including:

- Improvements to world generation, biomes, and exploration.
- More structures, abandoned towns, suburbs, and eventually massive cities.
- New survival mechanics and environmental systems.
- Continued development of the Wilderness Odyssey API.
- Performance improvements and overall stability.
- More immersive environments and atmospheric effects.
- Expanding the storyline and making the world feel more alive.

Some of these systems are still experimental, and not everything will necessarily be included in the next update.

### 🧪 Upcoming Limited Playtest

I'm planning on hosting a **limited-invite playtest for a short period sometime soon!**

The main goal of this playtest is to collect information about how Wilderness Odyssey performs on different computers and identify problems that might not show up during my own testing.

This will include collecting and reviewing things such as:

- Performance reports using Spark.
- Game logs and crash reports.
- Memory usage, TPS, and other performance information.
- Bugs, compatibility issues, and general stability.
- Player feedback about exploration, survival, and gameplay.

The reason I'm limiting invitations is because I want to be able to properly manage and analyze the information collected rather than opening it up to everyone at once.

**A few important things about the playtest:**

**World Compatibility:** Your playtest worlds may be compatible with future updates, depending on what changes I make to world generation. If an update requires a new world, I'll make sure to mention it in the changelog. I would still recommend backing up your worlds.

**Feedback and Suggestions:** Any bugs, feedback, or suggestions should be submitted through the appropriate support channels in the Discord server.

**Minimum Requirements:** At least **6 GB of RAM allocated to Minecraft** and a reasonably capable dedicated GPU are recommended as the starting requirements for testing. These requirements may change as I collect more performance data.

**Limited Access:** This will be invitation-only, and I'll share more information about participation closer to the playtest.

Please remember that this is an early playtest, not a finished release. There will likely be bugs and incomplete features.

I'll also explain what diagnostic information is collected and how it's used before testing begins.

### 🚀 What's next?

Right now, my biggest priority is building a solid foundation for Wilderness Odyssey so I can expand it without having to constantly rebuild or fix older systems.

I want this to be a modpack that feels like its own game rather than just another Minecraft modpack.

There's still a lot I want to accomplish, but I'm trying to balance adding new content with making sure everything actually works and performs well.

I'm also developing this alongside college and other responsibilities, so progress might sometimes be slower than expected.

**I don't have an exact release date for the next update**, and I don't want to promise one until I'm confident it's ready.

I'll continue sharing progress, changes, and information about the upcoming playtest as development continues.

Thank you to everyone who's been following Wilderness Odyssey and being patient with the development process. I really appreciate it.

I'm excited to see where this project goes and especially excited to start getting more people involved in testing it!
