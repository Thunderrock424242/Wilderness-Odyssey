## The Fracture and the collapse

Clean-energy research expanded into dimensional experimentation under increasing secrecy. Researchers warned of instability, but a major test continued. A meteor crossed the resulting fracture, creating the one true space-time rift, then struck Earth and destroyed everything within a 1,000-mile radius.

Impact devastation and infrastructure failure came first. Later, microscopic meteor-derived material spread through the atmosphere, rain, groundwater, and ecosystems. Its effects varied: some organisms died, some changed harmfully, and others adapted. It was not automatically a replicating pathogen or universally lethal toxin. Organized civilization largely collapsed over roughly 1–2 years.

## Eden, Reservoir-9, and the awakening

Project Eden began before the catastrophe as a classified preservation, spaceflight, and emergency-continuity program. Most occupants were eventually evacuated. Reservoir-9 was a separate water-storage, purification, and research complex that helped sustain emergency infrastructure connected to Eden. It did not cause the Fracture.

The player was an ordinary civilian, medically unstable after exposure to meteor-derived material. Their experimental preservation chamber could not safely be disconnected or transported. They remained behind for medical and technical reasons. The precise exposure route remains unresolved.

Aether, Eden’s facility-management and preservation AI, sustained the chamber for roughly 150 years. Biological effects, experimental technology, and Aether’s interventions all contributed to survival. Its records are damaged, and its reason for prioritizing this occupant remains unknown. An unidentified signal prompts the awakening; its source has not been established.

The player reaches a living, altered Earth and can survive, build, research, explore, and revisit Eden at their own pace.

## The evacuation records

Independent journalists Beal and Nathan documented blackouts, classified research, and ordinary lives during the collapse. Their companions were Oliver the dog and Peppermint the lizard. All four reached evacuation boarding. The early records leave their departure outcome uncertain. Their story is discovered through historical evidence, never through living present-day appearances by the journalists.

## Three worlds, an unfinished future

Some evacuation ships reached Mars, the intended refuge. Other late departures accidentally passed through the same original rift to Echo Earth. The rift then became dormant, leaving a residual space-time scar.

Mars developed advanced technology within harsh, resource-intensive habitats. Echo Earth became an accidental human refuge with varied settlements, some thriving, some struggling, and some abandoned. A loose council coordinates shared concerns while settlements retain local autonomy. Views of renewed contact differ; communication and scientific investigation are initially allowed while physical travel is restricted.

Reconnection proceeds through recovered telemetry, faint intermittent signals, and careful unmanned research. The main-story milestone is reliable communication and regulated scientific contact among Earth, Mars, and Echo Earth. Broader human travel remains later research subject to safety and permission.

The world continues after that milestone: building, exploration, restoration, cooperation, and optional discoveries remain available. The unique lore-level rift does not prohibit vanilla Minecraft portals or unrelated gameplay travel mechanics.
