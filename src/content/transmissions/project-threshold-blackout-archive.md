Project Threshold was supposed to stabilize a classified wormhole. Instead, its particle accelerator activated meteor material buried beneath the facility and opened a wound into Echo Earth — a warped reflection of our own world.

On The Siren Night, prehistoric life, anomaly storms, and broken pieces of reality came through. Humanity answered with Exodus Protocol and fled to space.

Years later, a survivor awakens in a forgotten cryo facility on an Earth everyone else declared dead. Guided by the damaged AI Aether, the survivor follows Blackout Archive symbols through abandoned bunkers and military ruins.

The archive carries a warning:

> The accelerator did not create the doorway. It activated the material beneath the site. The doorway led to Earth — the same world, remembered incorrectly. Echo Earth.

Beal and Nathan preserved the proof. The goal is not to restore the old Earth. It is to prove humanity can survive the one that remains.
