These terms apply when you join a closed or limited Wilderness Odyssey playtest, accept a playtest gate in Discord, receive a test build, or submit playtest reports.

This is a community playtest agreement, not a lawyer-reviewed commercial contract. If the project becomes public, monetized, or large-scale, have a qualified lawyer review it.

## 1. Who can participate

To participate in a playtest, you must:

- Be allowed to use Discord and Minecraft under their own rules.
- Be at least 13 years old.
- Have permission from a parent or guardian if you are under the age of majority where you live.
- Follow the Wilderness Odyssey Discord rules and staff instructions.
- Complete Minecraft verification if the playtest requires it.

Do not join the playtest if you cannot agree to these terms.

## 2. What the playtest is

The playtest gives selected testers temporary access to an unreleased or experimental build of Wilderness Odyssey.

The test build may include bugs, crashes, missing content, broken balance, world corruption, performance problems, or unfinished features. Back up any Minecraft worlds before testing. Do not use worlds you care about unless staff clearly says the build is safe for normal play.

## 3. Your access

We give you a limited, personal, revocable permission to download and use the playtest build only for testing and feedback.

You may not:

- Share the playtest ZIP, download link, private channel access, or private instructions outside approved playtest areas.
- Reupload, sell, or redistribute the playtest build.
- Claim the modpack, assets, code, or test build as your own.
- Use the playtest to harass users, attack servers, bypass restrictions, or abuse exploits.
- Publicly post private unreleased details, screenshots, videos, or spoilers unless staff says it is allowed.

Staff may remove playtest access at any time, especially for rule violations, leaked builds, abusive behavior, or unsafe testing.

## 4. Feedback and reports

By submitting bug reports, crash reports, feedback, suggestions, Spark links, screenshots, logs, or other playtest notes, you give the Wilderness Odyssey team permission to use that information to:

- Debug and improve the modpack.
- Reproduce and verify issues.
- Add items to known issues, upcoming fixes, changelogs, or internal planning.
- Discuss the report with staff, developers, and support helpers.

You are not owed payment, credit, ownership, or approval rights for changes made from your feedback. Staff may edit, summarize, merge, close, or reject reports.

## 5. Public and private reports

Some playtest reports may become public forum posts inside the Discord server so other testers can add details or confirm the issue.

Use private staff tickets or staff instructions for:

- Security issues.
- Serious exploits.
- Personal account problems.
- Reports that include private information.
- Anything staff asks you not to post publicly.

## 6. Privacy

Playtesting uses the Wilderness Odyssey privacy policy. The bot may record that you accepted these terms and the privacy policy before sending the playtest package link.

Do not upload secrets, passwords, tokens, private files, or personal information in crash logs or support tickets. Logs are redacted on a best-effort basis, but you are still responsible for checking what you submit.

## 7. No warranty

Playtest builds are provided as-is for testing. We do not promise that the build will work, be stable, be compatible with your system, preserve worlds, or stay available.

You accept the risk of crashes, data loss, performance problems, broken saves, and other testing issues.

## 8. Third-party services

The playtest may involve Discord, Minecraft, CurseForge or another launcher, Spark, a file host, and other services. Those services have their own rules and privacy practices.

You are responsible for following the rules of those services.

## 9. Changes

We may update these terms as the playtest changes. If the terms change in an important way, staff should post the updated version before asking testers to continue.

Continuing to participate after updated terms are posted means you accept the updated terms.

## 10. Questions

If you have questions about these terms, ask staff before accepting the playtest gate or downloading the test build.
