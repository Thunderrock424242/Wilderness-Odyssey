# Wilderness Odyssey Privacy Policy

Effective date: TODO
Contact: TODO

This privacy policy explains what the Wilderness Odyssey Discord bot and playtest process collect, why it is collected, and how it is used.

This is a community privacy policy template, not lawyer-reviewed legal advice. If the project becomes public, monetized, or large-scale, have a qualified lawyer review it.

## 1. Short version

We collect only what is needed to run support, bug reporting, crash review, suggestions, Minecraft verification, and playtest access.

We do not sell personal information.

We do not collect Discord tokens, passwords, private files, payment details, or background Minecraft telemetry.

## 2. Who this is for

The playtest and support bot are not intended for children under 13.

If you are under 13, do not use the playtest bot, submit reports, link a Minecraft account, or request playtest access.

If you are under the age of majority where you live, you should have permission from a parent or guardian before participating.

## 3. Information the bot may collect

The bot may collect and store:

- Discord user ID, username, and timestamps.
- Report text you submit through bug, crash, performance, feedback, suggestion, Spark, Q&A, or support forms.
- Discord channel IDs, message IDs, message links, and report IDs needed to track reports.
- Uploaded crash reports or latest.log files that you choose to submit.
- Redacted log excerpts created from uploaded logs.
- Screenshot or attachment links that you choose to submit.
- Suggestion votes.
- Playtest session details, checklist answers, final notes, and linked report IDs.
- Playtest release acceptance records showing that you accepted the terms/privacy gate.
- Minecraft verification link codes, Minecraft UUID, and Minecraft account name when you choose to link your account.
- Staff actions such as report status changes, claims, known issue entries, and changelog entries.

## 4. Information the bot does not intentionally collect

The bot does not intentionally collect:

- Discord account passwords or tokens.
- Minecraft account passwords or Microsoft account credentials.
- Payment information.
- Private Discord direct messages.
- Whole-computer files or folders.
- Background gameplay telemetry.
- IP addresses, except where a third-party service used by the project may process them separately.

Uploaded logs may sometimes contain private information from your computer, launcher, mod list, or file paths. The bot redacts common sensitive values on a best-effort basis, but no automated redaction is perfect.

## 5. Why we collect this information

We use this information to:

- Run the support hub.
- Create and track bug, crash, feedback, suggestion, performance, and Spark reports.
- Diagnose crashes and modpack issues.
- Verify that playtesters linked their Minecraft account.
- Gate playtest ZIP delivery behind terms/privacy acceptance.
- Prevent duplicate reports and maintain known issues/upcoming fixes.
- Let staff follow up with users.
- Improve Wilderness Odyssey.
- Keep the Discord server and playtest safe.

## 6. Who can see it

Visibility depends on where the information is submitted:

- Public bug, crash, feedback, and suggestion forum posts may be visible to server members.
- Private support tickets are visible to the user, the bot, and configured staff/support roles.
- Staff-only channels, bot logs, database records, and moderation notes are visible to authorized staff.
- A submitted Spark link may be viewable through Spark's own website.
- Discord attachment links may be handled by Discord.

Do not submit information you are not comfortable sharing with staff or, for public report flows, the relevant Discord forum audience.

## 7. Third-party services

The project may use third-party services, including:

- Discord for server messages, channels, files, identities, buttons, and slash commands.
- Minecraft/Microsoft account systems for Minecraft identity outside the bot.
- CurseForge or another launcher/file host for playtest package distribution.
- Spark for performance report links submitted by testers.
- Sentry if enabled for hosted error tracking.
- Prometheus-compatible metrics tooling if enabled for bot monitoring.

Those services have their own terms and privacy practices.

## 8. Data retention

We keep report and playtest records as long as they are useful for support, debugging, moderation, known issues, changelogs, or project history.

Temporary Minecraft link codes expire after the configured time limit. A completed Minecraft account link stays stored until it is replaced, removed, or staff deletes it.

You can remove your linked Minecraft account with:

```text
/minecraft unlink
```

You may ask staff to delete or anonymize your support/playtest data. Some records may be kept when needed for moderation, abuse prevention, security, project history, or technical debugging.

## 9. Security

The bot stores its local database on the bot host. Staff should limit access to the bot host, database, tokens, staff channels, and private ticket channels.

The Minecraft playtest client must never contain the Discord bot token. Minecraft verification should use the bot's verification API, not direct Discord bot credentials.

## 10. Your choices

You can choose not to submit optional reports, logs, screenshots, Spark links, suggestions, or feedback.

You can choose not to link your Minecraft account, but some playtests may require Minecraft verification before access.

You can ask staff questions before accepting a playtest gate.

## 11. Changes

We may update this policy as the bot, playtest, or support process changes. Staff should post the updated policy before relying on major new data collection or new playtest requirements.

## 12. Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact staff in the Wilderness Odyssey Discord server.
